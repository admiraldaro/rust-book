# Code of Conduct

Rust-Book aims to be a practical, respectful project for people running small RustDesk-compatible installations.

Participants are expected to:

- be kind and constructive;
- assume good faith;
- keep discussions focused on the project;
- avoid harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language;
- respect privacy and never post real credentials, logs, keys, or private deployment data.

Project maintainers may remove harmful content and may limit participation by people who repeatedly ignore these expectations.
